Welcome to TreeTrack.

Who we are

A movement built around visible impact.

TreeTrack is an environmental initiative that brings people closer to real-world action. Instead of letting a contribution disappear into a single transaction, the journey continues through planting, care, and updates.

Our first campaign turns selected product purchases into support for tree planting in Jakarta. The people who participate can later follow a virtual tree journey connected to that campaign.

02 · TreeTrack #1

One campaign. Many small actions.

Our first campaign invites people to shop three simple products while contributing to a shared end-of-month tree planting effort in Jakarta.

Campaign #1

Grow a greener Jakarta.

20% of profit from TreeTrack #1 products is allocated to the tree-planting campaign. After planting, participating supporters can access their own virtual tree journey.
